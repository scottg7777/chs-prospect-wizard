# Rep email template (prospect discovery link)

The rep console fills this in automatically (prospect first name, practice, link, expiry date) and offers
"Copy email text" and "Open in my email app" buttons. Reps send it from their own mailbox; the app never sends email.

**Subject:** A quick, skippable checklist for your credentialing & billing quote

Hi {first name},

Thanks for talking with me about {practice name}. To put together an accurate credentialing and billing quote, I made you a short guided checklist. It takes about 5 minutes:

{secure link}

Every question is optional. If you don't know an answer or haven't decided yet, just tap "Not sure" and keep going. Your answers save as you go, so you can stop and come back with the same link. It doesn't ask for any patient information.

The link is just for you and works until {expiry date}. Once you're done, I'll follow up with your quote and next steps.

Warm regards,
